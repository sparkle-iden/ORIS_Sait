Early Access Game

Get instant access and start playing; get involved with this game as it develops.

Note: Games in Early Access are not complete and may or may not change further. If you are not excited to play this game in its current state, then you should wait to see if the game progresses further in development. Learn more

What the developers have to say:

Why Early Access?

“Early Access allows us to develop Tyr in partnership with our community. We believe that player feedback is essential to creating the best possible experience, and Early Access gives us the opportunity to iterate on core gameplay mechanics, balance, and content based on real player experiences.

Our core gameplay loop is already fun and engaging, but we want to ensure that every system feels polished and meaningful before our full launch. By releasing into Early Access, we can gather valuable feedback, identify the areas that need the most attention, and polish the parts that matter the most.”

Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

“We anticipate being in Early Access for about 12 months before moving to full release. However, we're committed to taking the time necessary to deliver a polished, complete experience rather than rushing to meet an arbitrary deadline, so we’ll adapt and communicate changes as we gather feedback.”

How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

“The core gameplay experience you'll find in Early Access represents what we plan for the final game to be, but with significantly more content, polish, and refinement based on community feedback. We plan to add more tanks, more maps, and game systems to continue evolving the gameplay.”

What is the current state of the Early Access version?

“The current Early Access state is stable and fully playable, offering more than 12 unlockable tanks and 5 maps. You can refer to the "About This" section here on our Steam page for more, as all features there are available in Early Access.”

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

“No, we plan to continue being free-to-play. All gameplay content is, and will remain unlockable, without paying.

We will offer a Founder's Pack once we enter Early Access meant to give our earliest supporters extremely good value for being with us from the start. You can expect the price of the content in that upcoming pack to be higher in the future.”

How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?

“We love talking to players and getting feedback because it truly helps us make the most interesting game possible. We’ve been aggressively playtesting the game with external playtesters as early as 5 months into development, and we want to maintain a close relationship with players as we move forward.

You can expect active engagement with players on our Discord server and seeing community suggestions making their mark on the game as we develop.”

About This Game

This is Tyr, a tank shooter where positioning, strategic decision-making, and mastery of unique abilities win.

TANKS

Tyr features a roster of specialized armored vehicles and pilots. You’ll take control of treaded and hover tanks alike as you angle & position for defense, and rumble and glide across the battlefield. 

CUSTOMIZATION

Each vehicle also offers its own tech tree and ammo & component slots, affording you the ability to upgrade and evolve your ride to suit your playstyle. You may even be able to change up your vehicle’s unique keystone tech to dynamically alter your personal meta.

ABILITIES

Each vehicle features a unique power that impacts Tyr’s moment-to-moment play on the battlefield. A tank may have the ability to blink several vehicle lengths forward or back, send out a sonar pulse to spot enemies, or overcharge its engine to ram directly into an opponent and deal massive damage.

ASYMMETRIC VISION

In Tyr, you and your opponents will only be visible when within range or while making enough noise. Deciding when to push forward and when to fire from cover could mean the difference between staying hidden and revealing your position to the entire enemy team, so play carefully.

STRATEGY

Tyr will test your mental mettle, patience, and teamwork, as deliberate play beats whoever can fire the fastest.

FEATURES

  • Multiplayer tank shooter - Engage in strategic team battles with heavy-armored vehicles

  • A roster of heavy armor - Tanks in Tyr each feature a unique armor profile that you can angle and position to deflect enemy shots in the heat of battle

  • Vehicle customization - Individual tech trees, ammo slots, and component upgrades for each tank

  • Keystone tech variations - Max level your tank to unlock meta-changing tech

  • Asymmetric vision - Position yourself correctly, and you’ll see the enemy and spot them for your team, while the enemy can't see you in return

  • Unique vehicle abilities- Each tank has a distinct ability that makes moment-to-moment play more high-stakes and interesting

  • Strategy beats twitch reflexes - Run and gun gameplay gets punished by careful positioning and slow, deliberately placed shots to disable enemies
