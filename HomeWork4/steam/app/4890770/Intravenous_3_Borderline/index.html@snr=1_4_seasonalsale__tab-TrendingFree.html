About This Game

INTRAVENOUS 3: BORDERLINE

Intravenous 3: Borderline is a completely free standalone prologue to Intravenous 3.

Nothing left to lose. No reason to hold back.

Once hired to protect a corrupt politician, Gideon has now lost everything. What remains is a trail leading back to those responsible — and a simple, uncompromising desire for revenge.

TWO MISSIONS. YOUR WAY.

Strike from the shadows or tear through everyone standing in your way.

Use darkness, sound, distractions, gadgets, and the environment to stay unseen. Observe enemy patrols, create openings, and take them apart without ever letting them know you were there.

Or abandon subtlety altogether.

Engage in fast, lethal firefights where positioning, weapons, ammunition, and split-second decisions can mean the difference between walking away and bleeding out on the floor.

A FIRST TASTE OF INTRAVENOUS 3

Borderline is built to showcase the major gameplay additions and improvements coming to Intravenous 3.

Experience deeper systemic stealth, more reactive enemies, expanded tactical options, extensive weapon customization, new equipment and gadgets, and the brutal gunplay at the heart of the series.

Whether you ghost through a location without leaving a trace or turn it into a killing ground, the game gives you the tools — and leaves the approach to you.

REVENGE HAS A NAME

Gideon survived.

For everyone responsible, that was the mistake.
