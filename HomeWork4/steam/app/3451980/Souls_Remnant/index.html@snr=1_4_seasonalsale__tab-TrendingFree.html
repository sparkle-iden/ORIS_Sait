Early Access Game

Get instant access and start playing; get involved with this game as it develops.

Note: Games in Early Access are not complete and may or may not change further. If you are not excited to play this game in its current state, then you should wait to see if the game progresses further in development. Learn more

What the developers have to say:

Why Early Access?

“We're releasing Soul’s Remnant in Early Access because we want players to be able to experience the vast world we’ve built, while helping us refine and shape it in the best way for the future of the game, as community feedback is critical to us in developing Soul’s Remnant!

Souls Remnant draws on the feeling of classic 2D MMOs like MapleStory, where you grind, socialize, party up, trade, carve out your own build, and participate in a vast player-driven economy. We want players who loved those games to help us recapture that magic while we polish and expand the game’s world over time.”

Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

“We estimate staying in Early Access for 6 to 12 months, depending on community feedback and how quickly we can refine and improve the content in the game. We want to take our time with development and get it right, rather than rush it.”

How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

“For the full version we plan to include:
- More end-game content: additional bosses with unique mechanics, more areas for high-level players, and more general end-game content.
- Further refined and balanced gameplay for all skill tree paths in the game, as well as further refining and developing melee, ranged, magic, and faith skills.
- Full controller and Steam Deck support (controller support is already in the game, and constantly being worked on and improved with community feedback)
- Deeper crafting, gathering, and life-skill progression
- Quality-of-life improvements: UI polish, clearer feedback, smoother and more intuitive tutorials, and more quality of life in general.
- More cosmetics and equipment items to customize your character with
- Large updates, potentially including things such as player housing, seasonal gameplay, and entirely new areas to explore
- Ongoing economy, drop-rate, and balance tuning based on live data

Our goal is to add more content to the game, and to continue to refine and polish the game overall, working closely with our community to build the best version of Soul’s Remnant possible!”

What is the current state of the Early Access version?

“Souls Remnant is a fully playable MMORPG and currently includes:
- A classless, build-your-own-character system: freely mix the Melee, Ranged, Magic, and Faith skill lines through stat allocation and swappable equip, skill, and stat loadouts
- 72+ skills across combat and life disciplines to unlock and level up
- Over 1,000 items, including nearly 200 weapons plus hundreds of armor pieces, headgear, accessories, capes, and auras to collect and customize your character with
- An extensive gear upgrading system for customizing and enhancing equipment, building your gear in any way you choose
- A full crafting system, allowing you to craft items from 700+ crafting recipes, customizing them in any way you see fit in our crafting minigame
- Life skills including fishing, mining, herbalism, and gathering minigames
- Nearly 100 monsters and 19 bosses to defeat, along with 3 unique dungeons to explore
- 200+ maps across multiple biomes, with lots to explore and 150+ quests to complete.
- A fully player driven economy, with both an auction house and player shop stalls.
- 10 NPC villagers to befriend and learn about through completing their requests or giving them gifts.
- Full online multiplayer with parties, guilds, friend lists, a channel system etc. Everything you would expect from the social aspect of an MMO!

Generally, all of the core content of the game is fleshed out and is playable. We have content we’d like to expand on and content we’d like to add in the future, but we are proud to say the game is largely a completely functional MMO!”

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

“We do not plan to change the pricing of the game during or after Early Access. The game will remain free-to-play, supported by optional cosmetic and convenience purchases through our cash shop that fund ongoing development. These are optional and not required to play or progress, though we greatly appreciate our supporters.”

How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?

“We work closely with our community to gather feedback that drives Souls Remnant's direction in development. We frequently use:
- Our official Discord server for bug reports, suggestions, and input
- Reddit and other social platforms for additional feedback and discussion about the game
- Patch notes and dev updates to communicate upcoming changes

Community feedback is critically important for us, and frequently helps us in deciding the best way to shape and grow Soul’s Remnant. As such, we plan to continue working with our community to build the best version of Soul’s Remnant possible!”

About This Game

Soul's Remnant is a free to play 2D platformer MMORPG by Chaomoon, with a focus on social gameplay and exploration. Customize your skills with our classless system, team up with others, and defeat monsters, bosses, and dungeons together!

Venture into the mysterious world of Soul's Remnant with friends or meet new ones along the way - just watch out when night falls!

Game features:

  • 2D platformer MMORPG with a cute and colorful pixel art graphics style

  • Large world to explore with lots of monsters, bosses, dungeons, and secrets to find!

  • Classless skill system and various stats to build your character with

  • Lots of equipment of various rarities to find and upgrade

  • Challenging dungeons and bosses for your (and your friends) to conquer

  • Guild system with various perks for you and your guild to unlock

  • Full crafting and gathering system, with many recipes to unlock and items to craft

  • Comprehensive quest system that provides various fun missions to complete for rewards

  • Befriend 10 villager NPCs and learn their stories! Each with their own unique personality

  • Lots of player-driven and party-based content, such as parties, party grinding, housing, and player shops for a full player-driven economy!

  • Full soundtrack of 40+ originally composed songs, all written, composed, and produced by Chaomoon

Made by a small indie team!

Hi, I'm Chaomoon! For 8 years (from 2016 to 2024), I developed Soul's Remnant alone as my passion project. Over years of hosting public playtests, the game developed a community of dedicated players, and grew to be something larger.

Now, working towards bigger and better versions of the game, the team has expanded, with Rajah joining the team since 2025 to help with future updates and development!

Developed with community in mind!

Community feedback has always been a crucial part of Soul's Remnant's development, so feel free to join our community and leave feedback for us, as we continuously work towards making the game even better!
