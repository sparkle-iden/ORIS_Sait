About This Demo

After three weeks of continuous iteration and debugging, the BreathBound  demo has finally reached a relatively stable version.

The demo currently features an old mansion map with three progressive difficulty modes: Normal, Hard, and Nightmare. We'd like to extend our heartfelt thanks to the over 130,000 players who have already downloaded and tried the demo—it's your feedback and support that give us the confidence to keep making this game better.

If you've played the demo, we'd love to hear your honest reviews. Tell us:

- Does the mansion map deliver enough tension and dread?

- Do the three difficulty levels feel well-balanced?

- How smooth are the multiplayer matchmaking and team invites?

- Which gameplay moments stood out to you the most?

- What areas do you think still need improvement?

Whether it's encouragement, suggestions, or constructive criticism, we'll read every word. Feel free to also share your completion achievements, playtime, and epic moments—help more players discover our game.

The demo currently supports public lobbies and friend-invite party systems. Grab your friends, step into the old mansion, hold your breath, and face the undead!

- 1-4 player cooperative horror: survival depends on teamwork and role division.

- Breath and sound exposure system: not just hiding from sight, but managing your breath and noise.

- Sightless zombie hunting: the corpse king does not rely on vision, but on smell and hearing.

- Chinese Hong Kong-style environments: ancestral estates, walled campus, public hospital, tong lau tenement buildings, and more.

- Objective progression and rescue loop: sealing, rescuing, hunting, and delivery actions create high-pressure pacing.

- Stream-worthy tension: running, gasping, collisions, bell ringing, and teammate chaos all make for memorable moments.
