What the developers have to say:

Why Early Access?

“We are Luca and Léo, two French developers who released BODYCAM at just 17 and 20 years old. What began as a small passion project has grown into a talented team united by one goal: to create the most realistic and immersive FPS experience possible, while also bringing something new to the community through a more innovative and ambitious approach to the genre.

In an FPS industry that can sometimes rely heavily on familiar formulas and past achievements, we want to take risks, explore new ideas, succeed, fail, but build the kind of game we would want to play ourselves: an FPS made by FPS gamers, for FPS gamers.

That is also why we chose Early Access. We want to build BODYCAM alongside its players. For us, the community needs to be able to play the game, share its feedback, and directly help shape its future.
”

Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

“We anticipate that Bodycam will remain in Early Access for several years. The duration depends on community feedback and the improvements we plan to introduce.”

How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

“There will be no difference. The transition will happen naturally once all the main features are finalized and polished with the community.”

What is the current state of the Early Access version?

“The current Early Access version of BODYCAM already delivers a solid, highly immersive tactical FPS experience, with major updates continuing to expand the game.

You can check the latest announcements and patch notes to get a clear sense of the game’s current state.

You can also try the game at any time and request a refund through Steam if the available content does not yet meet your expectations.”

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

“The price of Bodycam could change after early access.”

How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?

“BODYCAM has always been built alongside its community and maintains a strong presence across social media, especially on Discord, where players discuss changes, share feedback and ideas, and play together.
”

About This Game


SEE COMBAT THROUGH THE LENS

Bodycam is the first ultra-realistic multiplayer FPS built to recreate the intensity of real combat. Experience tactical firefights through the lens of a body camera, where every angle you hold, every decision you make, and every bullet you fire can decide the outcome. Miss a check, and you pay for it. No crosshair. No ammo counter. No HUD. Countless hours were spent studying real footage, optics, camera movement, compression, exposure, sound behavior, and combat situations to push realism as far as possible.


THE TRUE FIRST-PERSON FEEL

Unlike traditional FPS games, where the camera drives the entire experience and floating hands are simply attached to your screen, Bodycam takes a completely different approach.

Your arms, torso, legs and weapon are all physically connected. On top of this, we developed our True Body technology, making every movement feel smoother, more instinctive and more natural. It takes a little time to adapt at first, but once it clicks, your weapon becomes an extension of your body and the line between you and the operator begins to disappear.

Once you've experienced combat this way, there's no going back.

WEAPONS THAT FEEL REAL

Every reload is driven by a dynamic animation system with separate calm and stressed states. Under pressure, your character can make realistic mistakes, rush movements and struggle to complete a reload, just like a real person would. Instead of playing the same animation every time, we created dozens of unique reload variations and mistakes, making every reload feel different.

ADVANCED WEAPON CUSTOMIZATION

Every operator fights differently. Your weapon should too.
Build your firearm from the ground up with hundreds of attachments and billions of possible combinations. Whether you prioritize speed, stability, or precision, every attachment changes the way your weapon feels and performs, allowing you to create anything from compact CQB rifles to long-range precision platforms. You define your weapon.

MODERN COMBAT TOOLS - FPV DRONE & RC CAR

Modern warfare has been transformed by FPV drones. Bodycam faithfully recreates authentic FPV piloting with realistic flight physics, Stabilized and Full Manual (Acro) flight modes, and advanced tuning options including rates, throttle curves, and flight profiles. Deploy FPV drones and reconnaissance RC vehicles to scout enemy positions, gather intelligence, strike targets, and support your squad.

PHYSICS-DRIVEN COMBAT

Powered by advanced procedural physics, Bodycam features a Ragdoll Stumbling system that lets operators naturally lose balance, stumble, recover, and react to the environment. Every movement is physically driven, making combat feel more dynamic, grounded, and unpredictable.

A WORLD WITHOUT MENUS

Forget full-screen menus. Your tactical tablet is your command center. Access your loadouts, gadgets, inventory, settings, and matchmaking instantly while moving through the Bodycam Shooting Range HUB. Customize your weapons, test new builds, practice your shooting, fly FPV drones, or simply spend time with other players, all within a seamless in-game environment.

BUILT FOR COMPETITION - BRING YOUR WINGMAN

Wingman is a tactical 2v2 Search & Destroy mode where communication, positioning, and precision decide every round. There are no hero abilities, no killstreaks, and no movement exploits. Victory comes from teamwork, map knowledge, and clutch plays alone.

FROM TEAMWORK TO TOTAL CHAOS - CASUAL GAMEMODES

Whether you enjoy competitive tactical gameplay or fast-paced firefights, Bodycam offers a variety of experiences. Fight for every round in 2v2 Wingman and 5v5 Body Bomb, jump into the chaos of Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch, Hardpoint, and Gun Game, or survive relentless zombie hordes in co-op.

SURVIVE ZOMBIES

Fight against relentless hordes of infected in an immersive, round-based co-op Zombies mode across multiple unique maps. Manage your ammunition, explore dangerous environments, unlock new areas, restore power, and survive increasingly deadly waves as the infection spreads.

GROUNDED PHOTOREALISTIC MAPS

Fight across highly detailed environments inspired by or replicated from real-world locations, from CQB buildings and forests to abandoned hospitals and trench networks.

NEXT-GENERATION AUDIO

An advanced audio system built around realism and immersion. Real-time sound propagation, realistic reverb, occlusion, and spatial audio let you hear the battlefield as it truly unfolds, turning every sound into a tactical advantage.

EARLY ACCESS, CONSTANTLY EVOLVING

Major updates continue to expand Bodycam with new technologies, maps, weapons, systems, and gameplay improvements shaped by community feedback.
