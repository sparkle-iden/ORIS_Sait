About This Demo

Can you guess your friend's tier list?

"Into the curry?" "What would you eat for breakfast?"

Rank five words from S to D based on the prompt, your way.

Everyone else tries to guess how you ranked them. Every correct spot scores a point.

The star player's tier list is always the right answer. Whether anyone else understands it is another story.

Every reveal ends with "Wait, you put natto in S?!"

[How to Play]

- Make your answer: Everyone gets their own prompt and five words. Rank them and lock it in secretly.

- Topic reveal: One player at a time becomes the star. Only their prompt and words are shown.

- Guess: Everyone else has 180 seconds to guess how the star ranked them.

- Results: The star's real order is revealed. Get all five for a Perfect!

[Two Ways to Play]

- Party mode: The star rotates each turn. Work together to guess their order.

- Streamer mode: The streamer is always the star. Viewers compete to guess what criteria they used.

[What's in the Demo]

- The "Food" prompts (2 word sets: Dishes and Ingredients, 74 prompts)

- Both game modes, plus creating your own prompts

- Japanese / English / Simplified Chinese

[Please Note]

- Online only. You need at least 2 players (up to 6). It can't be played solo.

- Demo players can only play with other demo players, not with owners of the full game.

The full game is coming November 2026. Please add it to your wishlist!
