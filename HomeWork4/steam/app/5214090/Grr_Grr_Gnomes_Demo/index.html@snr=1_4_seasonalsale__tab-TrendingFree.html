About This Demo

Grr Grr Gnomes is a co-op adventure about tiny gnomes stuck on a very big ocean.

You have a ship, you have your friends and you have absolutely no idea what you're doing.

Sail across the ocean and look for islands that hopefully contain something useful.

Fuel? Great.

Food? Nice.

A horrible creature that immediately starts chasing you? Also possible.

The islands have residents.

Most of them are not happy to see you.

Fight strange creatures, run away from bigger strange creatures, rescue your friends when they do something stupid, and try not to lose half the crew because someone wanted to "check something real quick.

Every island gets you a little further across the ocean.

Explore weird places, survive increasingly questionable situations, and keep sailing until you finally figure out how to get home.

Or don't.

The gnomes are doing their best.
