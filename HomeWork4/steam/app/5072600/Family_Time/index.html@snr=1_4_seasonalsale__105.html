About This Game

You're a Father Now

All of a sudden you have to take care of dozens of wolf children! Every night their number grows! Fail to do so and your partner, the Wolf Mama, will kill you! How many can you sustain?

Shelter Your Family!

Build houses for them, or command them to build them for you!

Feed your Family!

Farm food for them or go out hunting with them!

Pamper your Family!

Keep them snug in their own beds to not anger the Wolf Mama!

Can you support 10 children? 100? 1000?                                    10,000?

Find out.
