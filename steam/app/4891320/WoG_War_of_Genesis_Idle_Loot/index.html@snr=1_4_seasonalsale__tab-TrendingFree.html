Early Access Game

Get instant access and start playing; get involved with this game as it develops.

Note: Games in Early Access are not complete and may or may not change further. If you are not excited to play this game in its current state, then you should wait to see if the game progresses further in development. Learn more

What the developers have to say:

Why Early Access?

“The War of Genesis: Idle Loot is an idle farming RPG you enjoy right on your desktop.

We believe that a trade-enabled economy (via the Steam Community Market) can only be tested and refined through the market flow and farming balance that real players create together. To go through the process of checking and fine-tuning farming and the overall economy balance together with you before the full release, we've chosen Early Access. We aim to gradually improve the game with reference to the community's diverse feedback.”

Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

“We anticipate roughly up to two months, depending on circumstances. This period may be flexibly adjusted based on how content development and community feedback progress.

Our goal is not to hit a fixed date, but to deliver a sufficiently complete experience.”

How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

“In the full version, we aim to broaden the content and overall polish by reflecting player feedback gathered during Early Access. The directions we currently have in mind are as follows:

- Content expansion: We're planning additional farming areas, growth stages, and a variety of content.
- Economy & trading: We plan to stabilize the player-to-player trading system and continuously fine-tune the economy balance.
- Convenience features: We plan to keep improving the desktop overlay UI and overall ease of control.
- Content volume: We aim to expand the overall depth of progression and things to enjoy.

The above may change depending on development progress and community opinions.”

What is the current state of the Early Access version?

“In the current Early Access version, you can enjoy the game's core fun—idle farming, character growth, and player-to-player trading. From real-time item collection on your desktop to trading between players via the Steam Community Market, the core content is playable.

During Early Access, we plan to increase content volume, fine-tune the balance, and raise the overall polish based on your feedback. Some content and features are still in development and may be added or adjusted.”

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

“The War of Genesis: Idle Loot can be played for free in both the Early Access and full versions, and some in-game content may be offered as paid items (such as passes).”

How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?

“We plan to communicate with players primarily through the Steam Community and Discord.

- Steam discussion boards: We continuously gather bug reports, improvement suggestions, and balance/farming-related feedback here.
- Discord: As with the Steam discussion boards, we continuously gather feedback such as bug reports and improvement suggestions.
- Announcement updates: We share changes to the game and how your opinions have been reflected through Steam announcements.

We aim to listen closely to the wide range of feedback you share and refine the game together with you.”

About This Game

A personal mini-battlefield right on your desktop.

No clunky game windows, no cluttered background maps.

War of Genesis: Idle Loot is an idle loot RPG with a transparent background that runs smoothly in a small corner of your screen.

A 3D hero sets up camp on your desktop, battling tirelessly by your side no matter what you're doing.


Just leave it running, and it gets stronger on its own.

No controls required.

Whether you're working or sleeping, your hero's growth never stops.

Trade acquired loot on the Steam Community Market.

Items you loot can be traded with other players on the Market.

Experience the thrill of trading your hard-earned spoils and filling up your Steam Wallet.

The real fun is in the loot drops.

As you run stages, gear pours in.

Weapons, armor, and even gems!

Build your own powerful setup through endless item farming.

Join an Alliance and grow together.

Team up with alliance members to tackle powerful bosses in Alliance Raids.

Multiplayer action with players worldwide.

Challenge global players in World Boss battles, test your hero's might in the PvP Arena, and chat with the community.

Watch your hero grow automatically, trade looted gear on the Market, and compete with players across the globe.

War of Genesis: Idle Loot. Right now, on your desktop.
