About This Game

Mortal Shell II is a standalone sequel to Mortal Shell that significantly expands on the original with unrestricted, adrenaline-charged combat, extensive weapon upgrades, and free exploration.

The forgotten Shells of eight lost warriors are scattered throughout Mortal Shell II’s ravaged world. Seek and possess these Shells, unleash their innate abilities, and discover the story of each by unravelling their secrets.

Mortal Shell II offers a clean, agile and grounded combat system, with options to think strategically about upgrading melee and ranged weapons.

Unrestricted by stamina, players exploit multiple tactics to shatter enemy posture and strike critically.

Mortal Shell II’s open world is compact, tightly interconnected and immersive. It is designed with respect for the player's time.

Those who wander from the golden path can discover new Shells, weaponry, upgrades, curiosities and more.
