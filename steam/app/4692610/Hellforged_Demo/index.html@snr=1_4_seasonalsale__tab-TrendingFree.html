About This Demo

Hellforged combines the fast-paced, addictive chaos of bullet-heaven action with the high-stakes tension of an extraction ARPG.

In this free demo, step into the shattered world of the Blackpyre for an exclusive first look. Battle through hordes of bloodthirsty demons, test powerful ability synergies, and master the art of extraction, where every second of hesitation could cost you everything.

Every run in the demo is a heist against the horde itself. Explore the map, crack open chests, and take on quests, but never lose sight of your health. Decide whether to extract early and secure what you've found, or push deeper into the horde and gamble for rarer rewards. Die, and you lose everything you gathered.

Take command of 2 playable classes in this demo, each forged with its own distinct powers. Get a first taste of the wildly different playstyles waiting in the full game.

Take control of RNG! Use the Arsenal feature to pre-select which abilities will drop during your expedition, then test early paths in the Eternal Tree to craft specialized builds and discover screen-clearing elemental combos.

Think your demo build is unstoppable? Channel gathered chaos to summon a lethal boss, then prove your mastery by surviving the fight and extracting with high-tier gear.

Every month, a new Trial puts your build through a different sin-soaked gauntlet, from greed to wrath and beyond. Climb the live leaderboard and claim exclusive in-game titles if you make it to the top.


This demo is just a taste of the chaos. Add Hellforged to your Steam Wishlist to fuel its development and be the first to know when the full expedition begins.
