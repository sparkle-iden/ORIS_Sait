Coming Soon To Early Access

The developers of this game intend to release as a work in progress, developing with the feedback of players.

Note: Games in Early Access are not complete and may or may not change further. If you are not excited to play this game in its current state, then you should wait to see if the game progresses further in development. Learn more

What the developers have to say:

Why Early Access?

“Scam With Your Friends brings together co-op teamwork, unpredictable conversations with fictional AI callers, and increasingly chaotic workdays. We want player feedback to help shape how these systems develop. Early Access gives us a way to learn which scenarios players enjoy, where the progression needs work, and how we can make the call-center experience more fun and reliable.”

Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

“We expect the game to remain in Early Access for roughly two months, with a full release targeted for mid-December 2026. This estimate may change based on player feedback, the development of content and progression, and the stability of the experience. We plan to share updates as the timeline becomes clearer.”

How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

“We would like to expand the variety of scam scenarios and fictional callers, develop the workday progression and office interactions, and improve the co-op experience. We also plan to refine balance, performance, and usability using player feedback. The scope and priorities may change during development.”

What is the current state of the Early Access version?

“The game supports single-player and online co-op. Players run a fictional scam call center, talk with AI callers in real time, use remote computer access, and work toward daily quotas across increasingly chaotic workdays. The core experience includes scam scenarios, workplace disruptions, and performance reviews from the boss. The game is still in development, so players should expect bugs, rough edges, and changes to balance and content. A broadband internet connection is required.”

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

“We currently expect the price to stay the same throughout Early Access and at full release. We have not finalized full-release pricing, and if our plans change as the game develops, we will communicate any change before it takes effect.”

How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?

“Players can share feedback, report bugs, and suggest ideas through our Steam discussions and Discord community. We plan to use that feedback to help prioritize improvements to the co-op experience, AI conversations, scenarios, and progression, and to share development updates as the game evolves.”

About This Game

KOLKATA, INDIA IS CALLING YOU AND YOUR FRIENDS!

You and your friends run a scam call center under a ruthless boss. Cleverly scam and manipulate AI callers with your schemes! Hack into their computers to access the personal information you desperately need.

HIT THE DAILY QUOTA OR FACE THE WRATH OF YOUR BOSS!

Every day you progress, you expand your arsenal of scams. Scam innocent people out of their life savings to reach your daily quota or GET FIRED!!! It is you or them!

BE PRODUCTIVE! AND TRY NOT TO GET HACKED BY SCAMBAITERS...

Stay focused on scamming and try not get distracted. Watch out for the feisty scambaiters or you might catch the worst computer virus ever!

KEY FEATURES

  • Online co-op with your friends or public lobbies

  • Single player

  • Hilarious real-time AI conversations

  • Ridiculous scam scenarios

  • Run-based progression with increasingly chaotic workdays

  • Remote access to victim computers

  • In-game paint app, screen recorder, and camera

  • Survive computer viruses, police raids, air strikes, and other workplace disasters

  • Heavily moddable content

  • Create custom personalities through JSON files and voice cloning WAV files
