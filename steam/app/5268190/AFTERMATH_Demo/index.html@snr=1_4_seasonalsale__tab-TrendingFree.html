About This Demo

Demo Information: The current version is a demo. It contains the beginning and early sections of the full game. The game is currently in development, and some content in the demo may be changed in future updates. The current demo does not necessarily represent the final quality of the game.

AFTERMATH is a first-person horror game primarily focused on walking simulation. Players will embark on a linear exploration journey, experiencing an oppressive environment, a tense atmosphere, and carefully crafted scares.

Story: On a night when there is no turning back, you return here to deal with everything that was left behind. Explore the rooms, search for overlooked traces, and complete what needs to be done amid the silence and unease. However, as your exploration goes deeper, a hidden truth gradually begins to surface. You must then confront the traces and consequences that can never be erased.

【Warning: This game contains frightening scenes and sudden sound effects that may cause discomfort. If you have a heart condition, epilepsy, anxiety disorder, or similar medical conditions, please play with caution.】
