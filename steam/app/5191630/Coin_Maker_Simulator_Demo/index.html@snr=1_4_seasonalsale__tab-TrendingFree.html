About This Demo

You used to manage the royal treasury. Now you’re part of it. The King locked you in the dungeon with no way out. You need to earn the trust by doing the job the king is 'asking' you to do. Making coins is your only way to freedom! Good news is, it seems like you have a talent for it and you ready to spend some time in this cosy place.

Every step of the crafting process feels tactile and crunchy, with feedback that makes you wanna do 'mmm, nice'. Enjoy the process while completing jobs the King is giving you and slowly earning your freedom!

Make some coins on the side and bribe the guards to get new workstations and tools that make you a better crafter and move your progression forward. Unlock new resources and ways of working with them to make better profit. The bigger profit you make, the sooner you'll get out.

Who says a dungeon has to be dark and depressing? Clean it up, add some lights, hang a few decorations and make it your own workshop that feels like home.
