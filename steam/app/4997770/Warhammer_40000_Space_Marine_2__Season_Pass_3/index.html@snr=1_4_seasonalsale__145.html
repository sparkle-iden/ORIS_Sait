About This Content

The Season Pass 3 grants new armour, weapon and champion cosmetics from your favorite Space Marine chapters and allows you to save money on additional Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 content. Content can be downloaded from the store or in-game upon release.

Immediately available:

- Space Wolves Champion Pack

- Exorcists Cosmetic Pack

To be released:

- Deathwatch Champion Pack - scheduled Q4 2026

- Mortifactors Cosmetic Pack - scheduled Q4 2026

- Imperial Fists Champion Pack 2 - scheduled Q1 2027

- Minotaurs Cosmetic Pack - scheduled Q1 2027

- Blood Angels Champion Pack 2 - scheduled Q2 2027

- White Consuls Cosmetic Pack - scheduled Q2 2027

- //REDACTED// Champion Pack - scheduled Q3 2027

- Crimson Fists Cosmetic Pack - scheduled Q3 2027

Requires the base game Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2.

Mature Content Description

The developers describe the content like this:

Gameplay consists of frequent combat scenarios with characters using their weapons to slash/stab enemies.
