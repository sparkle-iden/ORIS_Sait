About This Game

🦸 Build the Ultimate Squad
Collect heroes with different roles: Attacker, Support, Collector, and Specialist. Each hero comes with unique attributes and abilities: 20 abilities, each upgradeable from level 1 to 10. Switch between three teams to keep the mine running nonstop.

⚔️ Forge Legendary Gear
Equipment and epic drops are your source of power. Reveal sealed drops, upgrade items from +0 to +15, collect stars by fusing duplicates, and chase the perfect attribute.

🌳 A Massive Skill Tree
A huge tree of upgrades and unlocks for your entire team, inspired by the style of Path of Exile. Specialize your build through dozens of nodes.

💰 A Player-Driven Economy
Rare items can be traded with other players on the Steam Community Market. Hunt for the drop everyone wants: the rarest finds become legends. We never sell power or loot boxes.

🗺️ A Journey Built to Last
Five difficulties, five worlds, 600 stages, all designed for months of progression!

Fair by Nature
Free to play. Just you, your squad, and the depths.

AI Generated Content Disclosure

The developers describe how their game uses AI Generated Content like this:

Some of the art assets in Bomb Farm - including character, item and environment artwork used in the game, on our store page and in Steam Community images - were created with the help of generative AI tools, and were then reviewed, edited and assembled by our team before being included in the build.

Bomb Farm does not generate any content with AI while you play. All content ships pre-made inside the game build; there is no live/real-time AI generation and the game does not connect to any external AI service.
