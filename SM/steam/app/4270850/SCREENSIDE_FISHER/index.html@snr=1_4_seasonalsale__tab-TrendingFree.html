About This Game

SCREENSIDE FISHER is a chill desktop fishing game you can enjoy in the corner of your screen.

Place your character anywhere you like and go about your work or daily tasks. When a fish bites, your character will signal you—and don't worry, the fish will wait until you're ready! Whenever you have a moment, reel it in through a timing mini-game.

Sell your catches or use them as ingredients for cooking. Combine various ingredients to discover new recipes, aim for higher star ratings, and complete both your Fish and Recipe Encyclopedias!

Use the money you earn to buy new fishing rods, upgrades, cooking ingredients, music, and ambient soundscapes. Meet shopkeepers and fulfill special conditions to unlock shops and bonus features.

Want to personalize your space? Use the built-in customization features! Decorate your character’s skin with your favorite images, and add your very own custom fish—setting personalized names, sizes, and custom artwork directly into the game.
