About This Game



FIND THE NEEDLE

Somewhere inside 5,000,000 individual pieces of hay is a single needle.

Your goal is simple.

Find it.

Unfortunately, finding a needle in a haystack is about as easy as it sounds.

START WITH NOTHING

At the beginning, it's just you and the haystack.

Pick through the pile piece by piece, searching for the needle and uncovering valuables hidden amongst the hay along the way.

Sell what you find and use the money to make your impossible task slightly less impossible.

UPGRADE YOUR SEARCH... OR DON'T

Find valuables, sell them and buy increasingly powerful tools that let you search, move and process larger amounts of hay at once.

The further you progress, the more ridiculous your search operation becomes.

Or, if you want the true needle-in-a-haystack experience, play with no upgrades at all.

No tools. No automation.

Just you, 5,000,000 pieces of hay and 1 needle.

FIND IT WITH FRIENDS

You don't have to suffer alone.

Team up with friends and search through millions of pieces of hay together.

More players. More tools. Same needle.

Good luck.
