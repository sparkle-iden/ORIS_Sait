About This Game

Become Shogun

Experience the updated, enhanced, and complete version of the grand strategy classic; Total War: SHOGUN 2 and all of its DLC, including the Rise of the Samurai and Fall of the Samurai campaigns. Updates include:

• Improved visuals, updated lighting, shadows, and textures

• 1440p and 4K UI scaling

• Modernised controls

• New full Japanese and Chinese voice acting and text localisation

• 50 + newly playable minor factions

• Integrated in-game mod support using Mod.io

• 64 bit upgrade, and more

Grand Strategy

Wage total war, unite Japan and become Shogun. Master Bushido and Chi, war and peace, build alliances, lead massive armies, construct mighty castles and execute acts of cunning subterfuge to fuel your campaigns across the entire Shogun era from the rise of the first Shogun to the fall of the last Samurai.

Tactical Battles

Command massed armies of samurai warriors in dramatic real-time tactical battles. Master the tactics of the Shogun era and sweep across the battlefields of Feudal Japan, commanding massive, pitched battles, besieging towering fortresses, and springing devastating ambushes. Glorious victory or shameful defeat will be yours.

Multiplayer

Create a persistent, upgradable general in Avatar Conquest Mode. Win online battles, earn XP and unlock better armour, new unit types, and permanent upgrades for your troops. Jump into Co-op or Competitive Campaigns to play through the entire Shogun 2 campaign by conquering Japan together or competing for supremacy. Up to 8 players can join hosted lobbies for massive, chaotic skirmishes in Custom Battles
