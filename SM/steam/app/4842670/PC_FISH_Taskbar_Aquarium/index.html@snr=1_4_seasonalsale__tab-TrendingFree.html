PC FISH’s First Season Is Coming!

Get Ready for Our First Season!

The first season of PC FISH: Taskbar Aquarium is planned to begin near the end of the first week of September!

  • 3 limited Seasonal Fish with unique animations

  • 2 new regular fish that will remain permanently obtainable

Seasonal Fish can only be created during their season through Merge, using the specified ingredient fish at the required star levels. They cannot be obtained through breeding or regular Merge. Once the season ends, they will only be available through trading.

Check the recipes and start preparing your ingredient fish now—the higher the Seasonal Fish’s star level, the higher the required star levels of its ingredients!

About This Game

Raise, Collect, Trade!

PC FISH is a cozy desktop aquarium that lives on your screen while you work, browse, or play other games.

Start with a few fish and grow your collection through discovery, breeding, and trading. Unlock rare species, find valuable traits, upgrade your aquarium, and build a collection unlike anyone else’s.

Every fish has value. Every discovery matters. Will you find the next legendary fish?

Trade Your Fish on the Steam Community Market.
PC FISH supports trading through the Steam Community Market.

Key Features

  • Free desktop companion

  • 450+ fish and decorations

  • Relaxing idle gameplay

  • Chill Lo-Fi background music

  • Steam Community Market support

A living aquarium on your desktop, always by your side.
Start raising your own PC FISH today.
