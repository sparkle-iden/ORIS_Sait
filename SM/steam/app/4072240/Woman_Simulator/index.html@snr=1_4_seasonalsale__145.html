About This Game

In the game, you will play the role of a housewife and experience all the household duties firsthand. Your husband will also check on you periodically.

You will have to cook, wash the dishes, do the laundry, iron and dry underwear and socks, mop the floor.

In addition, you need to monitor your morale and your husband's mood. Take a bath to relax after cleaning, flatter your husband to cheer him up.
