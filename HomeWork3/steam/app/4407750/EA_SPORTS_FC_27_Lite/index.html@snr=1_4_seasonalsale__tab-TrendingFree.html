About This Demo

Welcome to EA SPORTS FC™ 27 Lite.

EA SPORTS FC™ 27 Lite is a free-to-download preview experience that lets everyone step into The World's Game. Whether playing your first match, sharpening your skills, or challenging players online, FC 27 Lite is a great way to start your EA SPORTS FC™ 27 journey.

Play Kick-Off with a selection of the world's biggest clubs, master the fundamentals through Learn to Play, or compete online in Friendlies and Online Seasons. Offering a taste of what’s new in The World’s Game, FC 27 Lite lets you experience authentic football while improving your skills against friends and players from around the world.

FC 27 Lite is designed as a preview experience. Players can seamlessly upgrade to the full EA SPORTS FC™ 27 game at any time to unlock Football Ultimate Team™, Manager and Player Career, and The Grounds, carrying their progress with them when they upgrade on the same platform and EA Account.
