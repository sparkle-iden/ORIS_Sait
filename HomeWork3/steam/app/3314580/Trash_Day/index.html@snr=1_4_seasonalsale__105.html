About This Game

Every raccoon dreams of Raccoon Paradise. Unfortunately, you and your friends are currently locked up in Animal Control.

Break out, steal a wagon, load it with the finest trash you can find, and haul it across six areas to get there. Played the demo? That was only one area! The full game adds five more, new obstacles, and a full soundtrack. Online co-op for up to 6 players. You technically can play solo… but the wagon is a lot for one raccoon.

TEAM UP

The path ahead is tricky and full of challenges! Push and steer the wagon with up to five friends and clear the path ahead. Lose a wheel? Put it back on. Flip it? Flip it back. Distract animals on their own turf!

USE YOUR RACCOON PHYSICS DEGREE

Use bungee cords, planks, and more to propel your wagon over, under, and through traffic, bear traps, rockslides, and a river. Good plan. Terrible execution.

SCAVENGE THE WORLD

Knock over trash cans, search dumpsters, and scour everything from Animal Control, through the wilderness, on the way to the junkyard for helpful items as well as valuable trash.

LIVE YOUR BEST RACCOON LIFE

Climb, scurry, and scamper your way through the world. Wear a hat. Stack your friends. Launch one off a seesaw. Play pattycake with a friend. Eat some junk.

Made by Off By One Games, a small team with over a decade of experience working together on Rocket League.
