Coming Soon To Early Access

The developers of this game intend to release as a work in progress, developing with the feedback of players.

Note: Games in Early Access are not complete and may or may not change further. If you are not excited to play this game in its current state, then you should wait to see if the game progresses further in development. Learn more

What the developers have to say:

Why Early Access?

“Invokyr is a cooperative horror game built around unpredictable board games, changing rules, monsters, events, and chaotic dice. With so many systems interacting with each other, player feedback is incredibly valuable to us.”

Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

“We currently expect Invokyr to remain in Early Access for approximately one year.
Throughout Early Access, we plan to release regular updates around new themes, with new monsters, events, items and features. This timeframe may change depending on the development process and what we learn from players.”

How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

“Our goal for the 1.0 version is to significantly expand the variety and depth of Invokyr. We plan to add new boards and themes, monsters, events, dice, cursed rules, items, upgrades.
We also plan to expand the narrative mysteries surrounding Invokyr and the Game Master, and continue improving replayability based on what we learn during Early Access.”

What is the current state of the Early Access version?

“At Early Access launch, Invokyr will already offer a complete multiplayer gameplay loop for 1 to 6 players.
Players will be able to explore several themed boards, survive more than 15 monsters and 20 events, discover and use more than a dozen different dice and cursed idols, purchase upgrades and equipment, and face increasingly dangerous combinations of rules as they progress toward the ultimate Invokyr Board.
The game also includes progression between boards, a shop, environmental secrets, and a rule system designed to make each game unfold differently.”

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

“We plan to gradually raise the price as we ship new content and features.”

How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?

“Community feedback has already played an important role in the development of Invokyr through our demos and playtests, and we want that relationship to continue throughout Early Access.
We’ll actively follow player feedback through Steam discussions, Discord, playtests, surveys, reviews, and direct community discussions.
Feedback will help us improve balancing, difficulty, onboarding, replayability, and existing systems, but also identify which monsters, events, rules, dice, and unexpected multiplayer situations players enjoy the most.
The Game Master may control the board, but the community will help shape what Invokyr becomes.”

About This Game

Every Roll Summons a New Surprise!

You’re trapped. The house groans around you. Cold. Dark. On the table sits a 1970s board game, dusty but humming with energy. You’d heard rumours, of course. About the board, the house. You look to your friends, and a voice, whimsical yet bitter, speaks: “Let’s play a game.”

Once a beloved toy, the Gamemaster now rules this abandoned house. Escape is possible but only if you play. You roll the dice, and another worldly sound echoes through the house. Will you win your freedom or be dragged into the darkness?

Beat the Gamemaster

Play the game and survive the unpredictable terrors, traps, and curses that are summoned. Reach the end and you’ll escape. Perhaps.

Face the Fear Together

Online co-op with up to 6 players. Work together… or leave someone behind. Death is not always the end.

Explore to Find Dice

Scour the house to recover the dice hidden within. Each can only be used once. Choose wisely.

Special Dice

Some dice offer powers to heal, hide, or hinder those who hunt you, but nothing comes without risk.

Try Not to Get Lost

The house is a labyrinth of rooms. Lose your way, and you may never make it back to the board.

Fear-filled Features:

  • Immerse yourself in a co-op psychological horror, built around a cursed game that is desperate to be played.

  • Face bizarre, unique creatures: each with their own way of hunting.

  • Traps, curses, and chaos await every turn.

  • The house has different biomes: jungle, ice mountain, oversized toy chest.

  • Survive to earn rewards… and unlock more formidable challenges. Will you play again?

Come, friends, and play.

Live or die, who could say?
