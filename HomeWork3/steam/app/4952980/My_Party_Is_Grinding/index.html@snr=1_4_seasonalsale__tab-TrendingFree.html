About This Game

Tuck a small transparent window into a corner of your screen — or anywhere you like — and let your party of five heroes keep fighting, looting, and leveling while you work, code, or browse. My Party Is Grinding blends the comfort of an idle game with the depth of a classic hack-and-slash RPG, right on your desktop. You're not grinding — your party is.

Lives on Your Desktop

  • A semi-transparent window runs right alongside everything else on your screen

  • Drag it anywhere, zoom in and out, pin it always-on-top, or click straight through it

  • Glance over between tasks, click a dropped chest, and collect your rewards

Five Heroes, One Party

  • Knight, Warrior, Assassin, Archer, and Mage — five classes, five distinct roles

  • Level up each class with stat points and a kit of active & passive skills — equip a loadout of up to four actives

  • Dress every class in unlockable costumes — wearing is purely cosmetic, so mix and match freely

  • Unlock new classes and expand your skill slots as you grow

Endless Loot, Deep Gear Crafting

  • Over 400 pieces of gear across 8 grades and 12 slots

  • Layered enhancement with gems, engravings, and enchantments

  • Forge, fuse, and craft at the Blacksmith; engrave, enchant, socket, and extract at the Mage Tower

  • Stockpile the random chests that drop in battle and open them whenever you like

Four Regions, 200 Battles

  • 4 acts × 10 stages × 5 difficulties (Normal, Hard, Nightmare, Hell, Inferno)

  • Stage bosses and powerful act bosses that hit back with their own signature skills

  • Auto-challenge farms your current stage on repeat — and you keep earning gold & XP even while the game is closed (offline catch-up, up to 16 hours)

Progression That Never Stops

  • Orb Circuit — a sprawling directional growth tree with 200+ nodes

  • Constellations — a second growth axis with powerful keystone bonuses

  • Collections & Factions — completing costume gear collections grants permanent, party-wide bonuses, and owning more costumes from the same faction makes every hero stronger

  • Pets — unlock them by slaying monsters and keep their bonuses just by owning them

Automated Combat With Real Strategy

  • Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, and Chaos elements, each with their own status effects

  • A layered mitigation pipeline of dodge, block, damage reduction, and armor

  • Downed heroes and revives, plus a live damage meter to track each hero's contribution

League Battles and Rankings

  • Defeat other players' parties in duels for honor

  • Climb through eight leagues as your rating rises

  • Earn daily rewards based on your ranking

Trade Items on the Steam Community Market

  • Deposit gear of Epic grade or higher and materials of any grade into your Trade Vessel to register them in your Steam Inventory

  • List and trade them with other players on the Steam Community Market

  • Ownership of sold or purchased items is verified by the server and reflected in the game

Server-Based Online Game

  • All progress, items, and currencies are issued, stored, and verified on the server — your party is always safe

  • Rewards, crafting, and trades are all decided server-side — client tampering and item duping simply don't work

  • An internet connection is required to play

Grow a party that quietly, relentlessly gets stronger — right beside your taskbar.
