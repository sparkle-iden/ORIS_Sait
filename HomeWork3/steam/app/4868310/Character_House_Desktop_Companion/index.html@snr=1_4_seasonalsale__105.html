About This Game

Mix and match hundreds of parts to create a desktop pet that's uniquely yours

Standing Mode: Just stands there... though it does wander around from time to time;

Sit-in-a-Row Mode: Keeps you company while you work, syncing with every keystroke;

Hiding in the Corner: Lurking at the edge of your screen, watching over you quietly without getting in the way!

Rich animations and expressions let every character show off their own personality

Earn rewards even while AFK! Pull, craft, and collect accessories — experiment with all kinds of outfits!

Now, bring your character home.
