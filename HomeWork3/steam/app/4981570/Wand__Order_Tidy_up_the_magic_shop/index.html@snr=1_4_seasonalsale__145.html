About This Game

A Spell Gone Horribly... Dusty.

Tasked with tidying up the workshop, you—the Alchemist's homunculi—tried to clean by casting a spell. A single mistake changed it to a tornado spell! It went completely rogue, trashing thousands of items across three zones! Chase down your creation and clean up its mess before your creator returns.

THREE ZONES TO SORT

Work your way through three areas, each with its own unique sorting challenges:

  • Zone 1: The Archives (~470 Items): Pick up the pieces of scattered fate by sorting dozens of different tarot decks back into their correct shelves.

  • Zone 2: The Wand Workshop (~1,300 Items): Sort magic wands crafted from various materials and belonging to different schools of magic.

  • Zone 3: The Alchemy Lab (2,200+ Items): Categorize a massive array of potions and ingredients in tin cases, jars, and glass bottles.

CREATE PERFECT STACKS

You don't need to worry about aligning the objects by yourself anymore... Just select the items of your choice and CLICK to pile them up  P E R F E C T L Y  into a neat stack!

 UNLOCK POWERFUL CLEANING SPELLS

As you progress, you will earn points to unlock actual, working cleaning spells to speed up your work:

  • Alchemical Vision: Highlight every item belonging to a specific set.

  • Magnetic Vacuum: Attracts and picks up any nearby items that match the one you are currently holding.

  • Auto-Place Magic: Instantly float your held items directly onto their correct display spots.

  • And more!

Upgrade your spells over time and watch your speed grow exponentially!

FEATURES

  • Satisfying Sorting Gameplay: Perfect for players who love the cozy, organized satisfaction of games.

  • Cooperative Clean-Up: Play solo or team up with (up to 3 more) friends.

  • Secret Unlocks: Solve simple puzzles to unlock movement upgrades that will help you in your quest.

  • Cleaning: Don't stop at sorting: swipe the floor, clean picture frames, water the plants and more!

Disclaimer: All 3D assets, characters, visual effects, particles, music, sound effects, etc were made without using AI. Tons of human hours were used in the making of this video game, we hope you'll enjoy it!
